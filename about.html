About Heritage Atlas

A digital humanities project prototype that organises heritage discovery through countries, cities, and individual places.

Research, structure, and accessibility

The site uses a global map and a browsable left-hand country list. Country pages show popular cities, city pages list attractions with thumbnails, and each attraction has its own detail page. This layered structure helps visitors move from a broad geographic view to focused information.

Vietnamese and Chinese place names are included alongside English names for selected entries. A future version could expand language support, add more precise metadata, include image credits and licensing, and provide verified historical references for each page.

Individual Analysis

Heritage Atlas Explorer: An individual analysis of the research, design, implementation and reflection behind the digital artefact.

Anh Minh Nguyen - Yujie Gao

Heritage Atlas Explorer

Research

I chose cultural heritage as the main topic because I am interested in exploring the history, traditions and cultural identity of different countries. My project focuses on Vietnam, China, the United Kingdom and Ireland. I wanted to create a digital resource that allows people to discover important cultural and historical places while understanding their significance.

Design

I decided to develop an interactive website rather than a traditional written document because it can combine text, images and geographical information. The interactive map was an important design choice because it allows users to explore different locations visually. I also organised the website into country, city and heritage-site pages to make navigation clear and user-friendly.

Implementation

I used HTML, CSS and JavaScript to build the website and organise its content. I integrated Leaflet and OpenStreetMap to display an interactive map with markers for selected cities. I also included images and descriptions to provide additional information about each heritage location. I tested the navigation and page layouts to improve the overall user experience.

Reflection

Overall, I think the project successfully combines digital technology and cultural heritage in an accessible way. One challenge was organising the information and making sure that the links and interactive map worked correctly. If I developed the project further, I would improve the visual design, include more reliable historical sources and provide richer descriptions of each heritage site. This project helped me understand how digital tools can support the presentation and exploration of cultural heritage.

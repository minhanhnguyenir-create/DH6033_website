Tra Que Vegetable Village

Làng rau Trà Quế

Located approximately 3 km northwest of the center of Hoi An and less than 20 km south of Da Nang, the Tra Que vegetable village is home to 220 farming households, 130 of which specialize in the intensive, rotational, and intercropping cultivation of vegetables across 40 hectares of land. Tra Que has long been renowned for its diverse selection of over 20 varieties of leafy greens and herbs—some of which, such as various types of basil, lemon basil, and perilla, are prized for a unique fragrance and flavor unmatched anywhere else. When combined, these herbs offer a harmonious blend of five distinct tastes: spicy, sour, sweet, bitter, and astringent.

Terracotta Army

兵马俑

The Terracotta Army, or the Terracotta Warriors and Horses of Qin Shi Huang (Chinese: 兵馬俑; pinyin: *bīng mǎ yǒng*; literally "soldiers and horses funerary statues"), is a collection of terracotta figures representing soldiers and horses, located near the Mausoleum of Qin Shi Huang. The Terracotta Army was discovered on March 29, 1974, near the city of Xi'an, in Shaanxi Province, northwestern China.

Thien Mu Pagoda

Chùa Thiên Mụ

Thien Mu Pagoda, also known as Linh Mu Pagoda, is an ancient temple situated on Ha Khe Hill, on the left bank of the Perfume River, approximately 5 km west of the center of Hue city (Vietnam). Thien Mu Pagoda was officially founded in 1601 (the Year of the Ox) during the reign of Lord Nguyen Hoang—the first of the Nguyen Lords to rule in the southern region (Dang Trong).

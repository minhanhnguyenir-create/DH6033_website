Hoan Kiem Lake

Hồ Hoàn Kiếm

Hoan Kiem Lake (Han-Nom: 湖還劍), also known as Sword Lake, is a natural freshwater lake located in Hoan Kiem ward, the center of Hanoi city. The lake has an area of ​​about 12 hectares.In the past, the lake also had the names Luc Thuy Lake, Thuy Quan Lake, Ta Vong and Huu Vong Lakes. The name Hoan Kiem appeared in the early 15th century associated with the legend of King Le Loi returning the magic sword to the Golden Turtle. According to the legend, while King Le Thai To was strolling on a boat, a golden turtle suddenly surfaced and demanded that the king return the sword that the Dragon King had lent him to fight off the invading Ming army. The king immediately returned the sword to the magic turtle, and the turtle dived back into the water and disappeared. From then on, the lake was named Hoan Kiem Lake.

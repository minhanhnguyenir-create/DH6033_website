Temple of Literature

Văn Miếu – Quốc Tử Giám

The Temple of Literature – Imperial Academy (Văn Miếu – Quốc Tử Giám) is a premier, multifaceted historical complex in Hanoi, located to the south of the ancient Thăng Long Citadel. The complex comprises the Văn Lake area, the main Temple of Literature – Imperial Academy grounds, and the Giám Garden. Its core structures are the Temple of Literature—dedicated to Confucius—and the Imperial Academy, which served as Vietnam’s first university. Surrounded by walls, the site is divided into five distinct spatial zones, each separated by structures featuring three gateways (a central main gate flanked by two smaller side gates). Moving from the outside in, the successive gates are: the Temple of Literature Gate, the Great Middle Gate (Đại Trung), the Pavilion of the Constellation of Literature (Khuê Văn Các), the Great Success Gate (Đại Thành), and the Gate of the Imperial Academy (Thái Học). Over its more than 700 years of operation, the institution educated countless talents for the nation. Today, the Temple of Literature – Imperial Academy is a popular destination for both domestic and international visitors; it also serves as a venue for honoring outstanding students and hosting an annual poetry festival on the fifteenth day of the first lunar month. Notably, it remains a place where students today come to seek "good luck" before important examinations.

The Royal Mile

The Royal Mile (Scottish Gaelic: Am Mìle Rìoghail)[1] is a series of streets forming the main thoroughfare of the Old Town of Edinburgh, Scotland. The term originated in the early 20th century and has since entered popular usage.[2] The Royal Mile runs between Edinburgh Castle and Holyrood Palace, two significant locations in the royal history of Scotland, and is almost exactly one mile long.[3] From west to east, and running downhill, it comprises the Castle Esplanade and the public streets of Castlehill, the Lawnmarket, the High Street, the Canongate and Abbey Strand. It is the busiest tourist street in the Old Town.

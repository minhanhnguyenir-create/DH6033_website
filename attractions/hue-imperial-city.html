Imperial City of Hue

Cố Đô Huế

The ancient capital of Hue, also known as Phu Xuan, served as the seat of power for the Southern Realm (Dang Trong) under the Nguyen Lords from 1687 to 1774. It later became the capital of the Tay Son dynasty, beginning in 1788 when Emperor Quang Trung—born Nguyen Hue—ascended the throne. In 1802, when Nguyen Anh ascended the throne under the reign name Gia Long, he also selected Phu Xuan as the capital for the Nguyen dynasty—the final feudal dynasty in Vietnamese history. Hue’s role as the capital of Vietnam came to an end in 1945, when Bao Dai, the last emperor of the Nguyen dynasty, abdicated. The complex of monuments in the ancient capital of Hue stands as a testament to the flourishing development of architecture and culture during the Nguyen era.

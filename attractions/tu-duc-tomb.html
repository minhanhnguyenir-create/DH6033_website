Tomb of Tu Duc

Lăng Tự Đức

The Tomb of Emperor Tự Đức (Chinese: 嗣德陵) is a complex of architectural structures—including the burial site of Emperor Tự Đức—situated in a narrow valley in the village of Dương Xuân Thượng (within the former Cư Chánh canton; currently the Thượng Ba residential group, Thủy Xuân Ward, Huế City). Upon its initial construction, the Emperor named it "Khiêm Cung" (謙宮). Following his passing, the site was renamed "Khiêm Lăng" (謙陵). Characterized by its elaborate architecture and picturesque, harmonious natural landscape, the Tomb of Emperor Tự Đức stands as one of the most beautiful mausoleums of the Nguyễn Dynasty.

Trinity College Dublin

The College of the Holy and Undivided Trinity of Queen Elizabeth near Dublin,[19] branded by the board as Trinity College, the University of Dublin,[20][21] and officially incorporated as Trinity College Dublin (TCD; Irish: Coláiste na Tríonóide, Baile Átha Cliath), is the sole constituent college of the University of Dublin in Ireland.[22] Founded in 1592 by Queen Elizabeth I through a royal charter,[23][6] it is one of the seven ancient universities of Great Britain and Ireland.[11] As Ireland's oldest university, Trinity contributed to Irish literature during the Victorian and Georgian eras and played a notable role in the recognition of Dublin as a UNESCO City of Literature.
